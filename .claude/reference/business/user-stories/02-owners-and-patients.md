# User Stories — Owners & Patients

Covers the customer (owner) and their pets (patients) — the core relationship the whole business
is built around.

## Owners

- As **front-desk staff**, I want to register a new owner with their name, phone, email, and
  address, so I have a record to attach visits and invoices to.
- As **front-desk staff**, I want to search for an existing owner by name, phone, or email, so I
  can find their record quickly when they call or walk in.
- As **front-desk staff**, I want to update an owner's contact details when they change, so future
  contact and billing stay accurate.
- As **front-desk staff**, I want to see all of an owner's pets in one place, so I know who I'm
  dealing with before they even mention which pet they've brought.
- As an **Admin**, I want to archive an owner record we no longer deal with, while keeping their
  historical visits and invoices intact, so past records stay traceable.
- As an **Admin**, I want to restore an archived owner if they come back, without re-entering
  their information from scratch.

## Patients

- As **front-desk staff**, I want to register a new pet under an owner, with its name, species,
  breed, and date of birth, so the clinic has a record for that animal.
- As a **Vet**, I want to see a pet's basic details (species, breed, age) before or during a visit,
  so I have context for the case.
- As **front-desk staff**, I want to update a pet's details (e.g. corrected breed, or if it
  changes owner), so the record stays accurate over time.
- As an **Admin**, I want to archive a patient record (e.g. the animal has passed away or the
  owner no longer brings it in), while keeping its full medical and billing history intact.
- As an **Admin**, I want to restore an archived patient record if it was archived by mistake.
