# User Stories — Staff Accounts & Roles

Covers who can log in and what level of access they have.

- As an **Admin**, I want to create a role (e.g. Admin, Vet, Front-desk) so I can define the
  levels of access staff can have.
- As an **Admin**, I want to create a staff account and assign it a role, so a new hire can start
  using the system with the right level of access.
- As an **Admin**, I want to update a staff member's name, email, or role, so their account stays
  correct as their job changes.
- As an **Admin**, I want to deactivate a staff account when someone leaves, without losing the
  history of work they did while employed (visits they handled, records they created).
- As an **Admin**, I want to reactivate a staff account if someone returns or the deactivation was
  a mistake.
- As an **Admin**, I want a staff member's password to be securely set and never visible to
  anyone (including other admins), so accounts stay safe.
- As any **staff member**, I want to see my own name and role reflected correctly wherever my work
  is attributed, so accountability records are accurate.
